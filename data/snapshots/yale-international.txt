International Students
Yale’s evaluates all applicants through the same whole-person review process.
Yale welcomes high-achieving students from every country in the world. All applicants, regardless of nationality, citizenship, or country of origin, are considered through the same whole-person admissions process. All applicants are evaluated without regard to their ability to pay and all admitted students and are eligible for need-based financial aid.
Students applying from secondary schools outside the U.S. complete the same applications and fulfill the same application requirements as all other applicants.

Documents in a foreign language
All application materials must be in English. If any of your documents, including transcripts and letters of recommendation, are in a language other than English, they must be accompanied by a certified translation. We are not able to accept any documents that you have translated yourself.
Standardized testing for international applicants
International applicants fulfill the same standardized testing requirements as domestic applicants. First-year and transfer applicants must submit scores from the SAT or ACT. Scores from other exams, including international leaving exams or English proficiency exams will not fulfill Yale’s testing requirement.

Financial aid
International students are eligible for the same need-based financial aid as domestic applicants. Yale’s financial aid offers for international students include allowances for housing, meals, and travel to and from campus twice each year. Financial aid awards take into account the relative difference between the U.S. economy and your home country’s economy. International students who enroll at Yale are not required to reapply for financial aid every year.
