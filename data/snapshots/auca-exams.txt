Dear Prospective Student!
We would like to remind you that applications for
admission
and
financial aid
have to be submitted
by June 12th, 2026 not later than 5 pm
.
If your scores meet the
minimum requirement
you can either fill out the application for admission
online
or submit a
hard copy of your application
along with all
necessary documents
to Admissions Office, room G20 in the AUCA main building.
To be admitted to AUCA, all Kyrgyz citizens
must take
the National Testing (ОRТ) and submit valid 2026 ORT certificate. ORT score (only general section of the test is required) must meet the minimum requirement set by the Kyrgyz Republic Ministry of Education and Sciences for private universities. ORT certificates of previous years will not be accepted. Advance registration is required to take the ORT. To register for ORT, please contact
Center for Educational Assessment and Teaching Methods
at (312) 664838 / 620470.
