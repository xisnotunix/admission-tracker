SAT Weekend October 2026–June 2027 Test Dates
At this time, registration is open for all students for all the remaining fall 2026 test dates and all spring 2027 test dates.
SAT Weekend deadlines expire at 11:59 p.m. ET, U.S.
Request Sunday Testing

request Sunday testing
and which dates are available.
